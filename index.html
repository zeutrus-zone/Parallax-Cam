Camera and layers
Tap one to select it and edit it below. Tap ◆+ above to record the camera, focus and every layer at the current time.
Pick a time with the slider under the preview, arrange your scene, then tap ◆+. Do it again at another time and everything glides between the two. Tap a ◆ on the slider to jump to it. The ◆ next to a single slider keys just that setting. Turn on ● Auto and every change you make is keyed for you.
